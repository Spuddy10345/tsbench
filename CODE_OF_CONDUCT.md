# Code of conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
In short:

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, or publishing other people's
  private information.
- Keep issues and pull requests on topic and constructive.

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and pull requests that
break these rules, and may block repeat offenders.

## Reporting

If someone is behaving badly, contact the maintainer
([@Spuddy10345](https://github.com/Spuddy10345)) privately through the contact
options on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled in confidence.
